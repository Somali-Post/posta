How to Address Mail to Somalia
Somalia is developing a national addressing and postcode system. This page explains the current interim format for mail routed through Somali Post.

Addressing mail today
BN1011 may currently be used as the interim postal code for mail routed through Somali Post. It should not be treated as the final national postcode system.

The strongest address includes the recipient name, city or town, district or neighbourhood where available, and a recognisable building, street or landmark.

Important telephone notice
A valid and reachable recipient mobile or WhatsApp number is essential. Somali Post may use it to coordinate collection or final handover.

Recommended address format:
RECIPIENT'S FULL NAME
HOUSE, BUSINESS, STREET OR NEAREST LANDMARK
DISTRICT OR NEIGHBOURHOOD
CITY OR TOWN
BN1011
SOMALIA
MOBILE / WHATSAPP: +252 61 XXX XXXX

Fictional sample label:
AMINA HASSAN
NEAR HAYATT MALL, TALEEX
HODAN DISTRICT
MOGADISHU
BN1011
SOMALIA
MOBILE / WHATSAPP: +252 61 XXX XXXX

Sender checklist
- Write the recipient's full name clearly.
- Include city or town.
- Include district or neighbourhood where available.
- Include a building, street or recognisable landmark.
- Use BN1011 as the current interim postal code.
- Include an active recipient mobile or WhatsApp number.
- Include the sender's return address.

What BN1011 means
BN1011 is the current interim routing code for Somali Post mail.

BN1011 helps route mail through Somali Post during the development of Somalia national addressing and postcode systems.

It is not a complete, geographically allocated final national postcode system and should not be presented as one.

After the item arrives
After arrival, postal and customs processing may take place before Somali Post coordinates collection or final handover with the recipient.

Sending mail to Somalia FAQs

Can I send mail without a postcode?
When a postcode is requested, use BN1011 as the current interim code for mail routed through Somali Post.

Is BN1011 used for every Somali city?
For now, BN1011 may be used as the interim code for mail routed through Somali Post, together with the correct city or town. It does not identify a specific geographical area.

Is a recipient telephone number required?
Yes. A valid and reachable recipient mobile or WhatsApp number is essential for collection or final handover coordination.

Does Somali Post provide home delivery everywhere?
Do not assume universal home delivery. Somali Post may coordinate collection or final handover depending on operational arrangements.

What should I write when there is no street name?
Use the clearest available landmark, building, business, district or neighbourhood information so the recipient can be identified and contacted.

P.O. Box Service content

P.O. Box Service
A P.O. Box provides a secure and consistent address for receiving mail through Somali Post. Contact our team by telephone or WhatsApp to confirm the requirements and begin an application.

What is a P.O. Box?
A P.O. Box gives a customer a dedicated postal box for receiving mail at a Somali Post facility. It can help individuals, families and organisations use a consistent postal address for items routed through Somali Post.

Customer support
Our team will confirm the applicable fee, required identification and collection arrangements when you contact us.

Who can apply?

Somali residents
Somali residents may use a national identification document or another form of identification accepted by Somali Post.

Diaspora applicants
Diaspora applicants may use a valid passport.

How to enquire
- Call or contact Somali Post on WhatsApp.
- Confirm the current requirements, identification and applicable fee.
- Visit the designated Somali Post office to complete the application when instructed.

What to prepare
- Full name
- Reachable telephone number
- Accepted identification
- Passport for diaspora applicants
- Business information where applying for an organisation

Tracking event explanations
- Posting/collection: The postal operator received the parcel from the sender.
- Arrival at outward office of exchange: The parcel reached the main export processing facility in the origin country.
- Departure from outward office of exchange: The parcel left the origin-country export hub and is en route to the next leg.
- Item presented to export customs/security: Export customs or security authorities are inspecting the parcel.
- Item held by export customs/security: Export customs/security temporarily retained the parcel for checks.
- Item returned from export customs/security: Export customs/security cleared the parcel and returned it to the postal operator.
- Item held at outward office of exchange: The parcel is waiting at the export facility before it can depart.
- Export cancellation: Export processing was cancelled, usually because shipping plans changed.
- Arrival at transit office of exchange: The parcel arrived in a transit country's exchange office.
- Departure from transit office of exchange: The parcel departed the transit hub toward the destination country.
- Arrival at inward office of exchange: The parcel reached the destination country's import facility.
- Held at inward office of exchange: The parcel is on hold at the import facility for operational reasons.
- Item presented to import customs: The parcel was handed to destination customs for clearance.
- Held by import customs: Destination customs are inspecting or processing the parcel.
- Item returned from customs (import): Customs finished processing and released the parcel back to the postal operator.
- Departure from inward office of exchange: The parcel left the import facility and is moving into the domestic delivery network.
- Item into sorting centre: The parcel arrived at a local sorting centre in the destination country.
- Item out of sorting centre: Sorting is complete and the parcel left the sorting centre.
- Arrival at delivery office: The parcel reached the local delivery post office.
- Item held at delivery depot: The parcel is waiting at the local delivery depot.
- Item out for physical delivery: A courier has the parcel and is attempting delivery.
- Item arrival at collection point: The parcel is ready for the recipient to pick up at a collection point.
- Final delivery: The parcel has been delivered to the recipient.
- Unsuccessful delivery attempt: A delivery attempt failed; typically a notice will be left.
- Import terminated: The import process was stopped; the parcel may be returning to sender.
- Item out of sorting centre: The parcel left a sorting centre (legacy code).

Tracking

What format should my tracking number use?
Use a 13-character S10 tracking number with two letters, nine digits and two country letters, for example RR123456785DE.

Why is my tracking number not showing any updates?
The item may not have reached a supported postal event yet, or the sending postal operator may not have provided an update.

When will a tracking event appear?
Tracking events appear when available postal updates are received from participating postal operators.

Where can I find my tracking number?
Check the receipt, dispatch confirmation or message provided by the sending postal operator.
